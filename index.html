About Us

Grounded in Rigor.
Applied to Reality.

Analogia Entis is an independent consulting practice specializing in the philosophical and formal foundations of artificial intelligence. The firm draws on deep expertise in formal logic, philosophy of science, and knowledge representation to help organizations navigate the conceptual and technical challenges posed by modern AI.

Founded by Hugolin Bergier — Associate Professor at Regis University and Research Scientist at Phase Change Software — the practice bridges two worlds rarely joined: the analytical precision of mathematical logic and the reflective depth of continental philosophy.

The name Analogia Entis — the analogy of being — reflects our conviction that sound reasoning about intelligence, whether natural or artificial, requires attention to the deep structure of what it means to be, to represent, and to know.

Philosophical Depth

We bring the full resources of analytic and continental philosophy to bear on technology questions — moving beyond surface-level ethics to address the conceptual foundations that shape AI design and governance.

Formal Rigor

Our work is grounded in formal logic, λ-calculus, type theory, and ontology engineering — providing mathematically precise frameworks where others offer vague intuitions.

Practical Focus

From research publications and granted patents to curriculum design and executive advising, we translate theoretical insights into concrete deliverables and actionable guidance.

Hugolin BERGIER

Founder & Principal Consultant

Associate Professor · Research Scientist
Philosopher of Logic & AI

Hugolin Bergier holds a PhD from Université Paris-Sorbonne and has held academic positions at Sciences Po Paris, the University of Chicago, and Regis University (Denver), where he is currently an Associate Professor of Computer Science and Program Director.

As Research Scientist at Phase Change Software, he has developed logic-based AI tools for program understanding, resulting in three granted patents (US, EP) and publications in venues including Logica Universalis, EPJ Web of Conferences, and AAAI. His philosophical work spans formal logic, philosophy of technology, and the ethics of artificial agency.
